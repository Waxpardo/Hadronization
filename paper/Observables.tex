\section{Heavy-flavour balancing observables}
\label{sec:observables}

\subsection{Signed species and ordered pairs}

Heavy-flavour sign follows quark content rather than electric charge,
\begin{equation}
 q_c=n_c-n_{\bar c}, \qquad q_b=n_b-n_{\bar b}.
\end{equation}
Thus a $B^+$ meson has $q_b=-1$, whereas a $\Lambda_b^0$ baryon has
$q_b=+1$. The signed species and pair registries enumerate particles and
antiparticles separately and define 300 ordered trigger--associate
combinations.\claim{O1}

Triggers are final, direct-primary, central-ground heavy hadrons with
$p_{\mathrm{T}}>1.0$~GeV/$c$ and $|\eta|\leq4$. Their heavy quark must resolve
to the selected hard process. Associates obey the same final-state,
direct-primary, central-ground, and pseudorapidity requirements, with
$p_{\mathrm{T}}>0.15$~GeV/$c$, but no selected-hard origin requirement is
imposed.\claim{O2} The reduction excludes self-pairs and pairs whose two
hadrons claim the same selected hard parton. This trigger--associate asymmetry
is necessary: demanding selected-hard ancestry for both particles would remove
the same-sign term by construction.

\subsection{Correlation and balancing yield}

For a fixed ordered species pair and multiplicity class, the per-trigger
angular observable is
\begin{equation}
 B(\dphi)=\frac{1}{N_{\mathrm{trig}}}
 \left(\frac{\mathrm{d}N_{\mathrm{OS}}}{\mathrm{d}\dphi}
       -\frac{\mathrm{d}N_{\mathrm{SS}}}{\mathrm{d}\dphi}\right).
 \label{eq:balance}
\end{equation}
OS and SS refer to opposite and equal heavy-flavour sign. The same-sign factor
is exactly one. Both terms use the same $N_{\mathrm{trig}}$, obtained from the
dedicated \pathcode{hTrKinematics} object. A trigger-momentum projection of the
pair object counts trigger--associate pairs and is not an admissible trigger
denominator.\claim{O3}

The stored angular axis is $-\pi/2\leq\dphi<3\pi/2$. The integrated balancing
yield currently supported by implementation and evidence is the full-axis
integral,
\begin{equation}
 Y_{\mathrm{bal}}=
 \frac{N_{\mathrm{OS}}-N_{\mathrm{SS}}}{N_{\mathrm{trig}}}.
 \label{eq:integrated}
\end{equation}
No reviewed contract defines near-side or away-side integration boundaries,
so this manuscript makes no regional-integral claim. A dated 21 August 2026
scope ruling confirms the full $\dphi$ axis as the only currently supported
integrated observable. Regional observables require a later signed decision
and a new contract; the ruling does not rewrite any earlier
preregistration.\claim{O4}

A baryon/reference-meson ratio divides two signed balancing yields. For the
paper-facing beauty quantity this is the $\Lambda_b/B^-$ balancing-yield ratio
for a $B^+$ trigger. The charm analogue uses the registry-resolved
$\bar\Lambda_c^-/D^-$ definition for a $D^+$ trigger. These are not inclusive
baryon-to-meson production ratios.\claim{O5}

\subsection{Central values and statistical uncertainty}

The central value is calculated from the complete pooled union of selected
files. It is not the mean of block values. Statistical uncertainties are the
standard errors of the complete estimator evaluated in ten disjoint,
equal-exposure file blocks. For block estimates $x_k$,
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{SEM}(x)=
 \frac{1}{\sqrt{10}}
 \sqrt{\frac{1}{9}\sum_{k=1}^{10}(x_k-\bar{x})^2}.
\end{equation}
OS--SS subtraction and every nonlinear yield ratio are formed inside each
block before the standard error is calculated, retaining the within-block
covariance of shared counts. Final points require ten finite block
estimators.\claim{O6}
