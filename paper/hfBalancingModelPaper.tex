\begin{abstract}
We study how compensating charm and beauty are distributed among heavy-hadron
species and in relative azimuth in a forced hard-heavy PYTHIA~8.317 sample of
proton--proton collisions at $\sqrt{s}=13.6$~TeV.\claim{A1} The observable is
the opposite-heavy-sign minus same-heavy-sign associated yield per selected
trigger, with the trigger count taken from the dedicated trigger-kinematics
object.\claim{A2} Event activity is measured with final charged non-heavy
particles in $|\eta|\leq 1$, and eleven common absolute multiplicity classes
are used for the complete MONASH, JUNCTIONS, and CLOSEPACKING tune
bundles.\claim{A3} Committed tables show activity-dependent redistribution
between beauty meson and baryon balancing yields in both non-baseline bundles;
the current uncertainty-qualified inference remains provisional because the
wide-counter systematic is absent and the tune-dependent generator-hang
selection risk is unbounded.\claim{A4} No mechanism-isolation claim is made.
The scientific figure programme remains blocked until exact ROOT-derived
bytes, ten-block coverage, numerical sources, receipts, and visual acceptance
are available for all manuscript figures.
\end{abstract}
