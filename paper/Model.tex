\section{Generator samples and event activity}
\label{sec:model}

\subsection{Campaign definition}

The canonical \pathcode{HF_RUN3_V1} campaign was generated with PYTHIA~8.317
for proton--proton collisions at $\sqrt{s}=13.6$~TeV. Both
\pathcode{HardQCD:hardccbar} and \pathcode{HardQCD:hardbbbar} are enabled, with
\pathcode{PhaseSpace:pTHatMin = 2.0}. The producer requires exactly one signed
selected-hard heavy-quark pair in every successful event. The sample is
therefore forced hard-heavy and is not a minimum-bias sample.\claim{M1}

Each tune has 1,000 canonical files with 100,000 successful events per file,
or 100 million events per tune. Ten disjoint statistical blocks contain 100
files per tune.\claim{M2} The three nominal cards contain the complete MONASH,
JUNCTIONS, and CLOSEPACKING bundles; no parameter family is interpreted in
isolation.

\subsection{Multiplicity counter and common classes}

Event activity is the operational ``final charged non-heavy'' counter. It
increments exactly when PYTHIA reports a particle as final and charged, the
particle has no heavy constituent, $p_{\mathrm{T}}>0.15$~GeV/$c$, and
$|\eta|\leq1$.\claim{M3} The cards use
\pathcode{ParticleDecays:tau0Max = 0.01}~mm; the installed PYTHIA particle
table contains no tested light-hadron state between that cutoff and the
conventional 10~mm primary threshold. Heavy-hadron decays are disabled, and
heavy hadrons are excluded because their artificial stability would create an
activity--observable autocorrelation. The counter should therefore not be
called an experimentally identical primary-particle definition without this
qualification.

A paired PYTHIA~8.317 MONASH minimum-bias calibration measured the production
counter at 6.986 and the intended experimental-decay convention at 7.040, a
0.7670\% undercount. Propagating that measured shift up and down through the
current half-integer boundaries moves no boundary across an integer, so the S5
class-membership change is exactly zero for c1--c11 in the calibration. This
does not establish the bias in the forced hard-heavy sample and does not
correct the percentile labels.

The analysis uses eleven non-overlapping absolute classes common to all three
tunes. Their half-integer boundaries assign every integer \nch to exactly one
class. MONASH minimum-bias top-percentile ranges are labels for this shared
axis; they are not per-tune quantiles and their direction is opposite to the
class numbering.\claim{M4}

\subsection{Systematic scope}

The registered sources vary renormalisation and factorisation scales (S1a and
S1b), the parton distribution (S2), the hard-process threshold (S3), the
activity-counter window (S4), decay-daughter class migration (S5), and
ambiguous hard-carrier assignment (S6).\claim{M5} S4 has no class-resolved
delta. S6 uses a different five-bin axis and is not added to the c1--c11
budget. Detector response, efficiency, resolution, and unfolding are outside
this generator-level study. The 2026-08-21 correction applies the documented
nominal-plus-variation SEM rule to derived deltas. Uncertainty-bearing final
figures and release-level inferences remain blocked by the missing S4 source
and the unbounded tune-dependent generator-hang selection risk.
