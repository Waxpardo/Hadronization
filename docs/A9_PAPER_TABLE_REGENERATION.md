## Why the attic macro cannot simply be re-pointed

It reads `hidCharm`, `hidBeauty`, `hidBc` and `hMULTIPLICITY` histograms plus a
tree named `tree`. The current raw schema is **`hf_primary_ground_raw_v7`**,
which stores per-event vectors (`heavyPdg`, `heavyIsFinal`, `heavyQc`,
`heavyQb`, …) and **has none of those histograms**. The macro is evidence of
what was counted, not a runnable path to counting it again.

## What it would actually take

**A counting pass over all 3000 raw files** (1000 per tune, ~92 MB each,
~276 GB), accumulating over `heavyIsFinal && q_sector != 0`:

- per-PDG counts for the eight species, and
- **Σ|q_c| and Σ|q_b|** — valence quanta, not hadrons. A Ξ_cc contributes 2.
  This is the distinction the table's own caption draws, and it is why the M7
  hadron counts cannot be reused.

**The minimal implementation is a sibling of the M7 macro, not new machinery.**
`Validation/MeasureUnresolvedSystematic.C` already walks exactly this population
with exactly this cut; it accumulates a baryon/meson split where this needs a
per-PDG histogram and two valence sums.
