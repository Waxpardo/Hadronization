## 1. ⚠ A PROBE THE PROJECT RELIES ON IS INVALID HERE — and fails as a silent zero

**The atime frontier does not work on merged outputs.** Measured: `atime > start`
returned **0 of 300 files in all eleven directories** while `/proc/<pid>/fd`
showed those exact files **open and being read**.

**Why:** every merged pair file is written and then read by the merge's own
`ValidatePairDirectory` pass before promotion, so its atime already equals mtime
and is minutes old. Under `relatime` a later read updates nothing. **The probe's
validity condition — files not read recently — is violated by construction for
anything the merge just produced.** It holds for `per_job/` inputs during the
gate, which is where it was measured and where it works.

**Use instead on merged outputs:** `/proc/<pid>/fd` (names the file being read
now) and `/proc/<pid>/io` `rchar` (monotonic, relatime-immune). Recorded in
`docs/GATE_3000.md` §4 — **the third correction to that method**.

> **A zero from the atime probe is not evidence of a stall. It nearly read as
> one here**, on a job that was 7 h in and working.

**I also misread the two PIDs before checking fds** — I had the merge and the
closure swapped, and briefly reported closure as "at block 4 of 10". It has all
eleven open and compares per pair-file. **The fd probe corrected it.**
