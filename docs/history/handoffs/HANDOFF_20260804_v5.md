### 4a. The owner's stated expectation was wrong in direction — say so

The brief expected that because `pTHatMin = 2.0` sits below all three `pT0Ref`
values, "the qualitative justification [should] hold for every tune and only the
quantitative offsets differ".

From the cards: `MultipartonInteractions:pT0Ref` = **2.28** MONASH (PYTHIA
default via `Tune:pp = 14`, not written in the card), **2.15** JUNCTIONS,
**2.194** CLOSEPACKING. 2.0 is indeed below all three — **but the margins are
0.28, 0.15 and 0.194, nearly a factor two apart.** The whole mechanism is that
requiring a hard process softer than the MPI cutoff selects quiet events, and
the size of that effect scales with how far below the cutoff you sit. So the
justification is **weakest for JUNCTIONS**, and the offsets are not guaranteed
to be a small perturbation around MONASH's −4.16 %. This is a prediction, not a
measurement — it is exactly what B4 will settle.

### 4b. Extension to JUNCTIONS/CLOSEPACKING — decisions taken, work not started

`CalibrateMultiplicityAgainstMinBias.C` hardcodes `Tune:pp = 14` and takes no
tune argument, so this is a code change. **Two decisions were put to the owner
and answered:**

1. **How the tune enters:** read the production card
   (`SimulationScripts/pythiasettings_Hard_Low_ccbb_<TUNE>.cmnd`) and re-apply
