|---|---|

### 3a. Why this kills it

1. **MONASH's 49x step is in (25, 50]. No MONASH sparse crosses in that
   interval.** The crossings are at ~11.5, ~12.9 and ~93. The step coincides
   with nothing.
2. **MONASH's (50, 100] interval contains three first-crossings and is the most
   sub-linear interval in the entire series (1.434x for 2x inputs).** A first
   crossing is therefore directly measured as **cheap**.
3. **JUNCTIONS' (10, 25] interval contains two first-crossings — its two largest
   objects — and cost per elementary merge *fell*, 16.99 → 12.54 ms.** Second
   independent demonstration that crossings are cheap.

**Also note the shape argument fails in the other direction.** MONASH's
`hTrKinematics` goes 1 → 9 chunks across the range; in (25, 50] it adds roughly
two chunks and in (50, 100] roughly four more, cheaply. Neither first nor
subsequent crossings track the step.

### 3b. What replaces it — nothing established, one candidate

**The cause of the 49x step is now unknown.** The constraint is sharp: whatever
it is, it is MONASH-specific, absent in JUNCTIONS across the entire range, and
switches between 25 and 50 inputs at flat RSS, no swap, CPU/wall 0.99.

**Untested candidate, recorded so it is not re-derived:** `THnSparse` bin lookup
uses a `TExMap` hash (`fBins`) with collision chaining. If its load factor
crosses a rehash/degradation threshold, find-or-create per bin degrades toward a
chain scan. MONASH has ~1.4–1.9x JUNCTIONS' bin count at equal inputs, so it
would reach such a threshold first. **This is a hypothesis with no measurement
behind it. Do not write it up.**

### 3c. The extrapolation risk is CLOSED — by measurement, not by mechanism

The owner correctly identified the dangerous branch: JUNCTIONS and CLOSEPACKING
have **three sparses each still single-chunk at 100 inputs**, so their crossings
sit at ~180 and ~194 inputs — **inside** the 100 → 1000 range the projection
spans. That is exactly the case that should block deferral.

**It is closed anyway, because the crossing event itself has now been measured
twice and costs nothing** (3a items 2 and 3). A pending event whose cost is
known to be ~zero is not a risk. Furthermore, **no object crosses after ~194
inputs** — at 1000 inputs every sparse in every tune is deep in multi-chunk
territory (MONASH `hTrKinematics` ~87 chunks), so the crossing landscape is
fully exhausted by ~200 inputs.

**Consequence for the mechanism plan:** v7 §9e's instrumented harness would
localise a step whose only proposed mechanism is now falsified, in service of a
risk that is already closed. **Recommend POST_SUBMISSION.** Owner had asked to
be consulted if any tune was single-chunk at 100 — they are, so this is the
consultation, with the reason the literal condition no longer binds.

### 3d. The instrumented harness is far cheaper than v7 §9e assumed

`MergeAnalysisObjects.C:362` **already emits per-input progress** —
`Merged i/N files into <path>`, every 10 inputs. **v7 §9e's claim that the macro
emits only `CANONICAL_MERGE_SUMMARY` is wrong.** The emission is suppressed
because `MergeCanonicalAnalysis.C:73` passes **`verbose = false`**.

So localisation does not need new instrumentation: **copy the macro to scratch
and flip one literal to `true`.** Piped through a timestamping wrapper this
