## 3. Item 1 — STOPPED ON A DEFINITIONAL DECISION. Read this before computing

**The observable named in the brief, "Σ_b/Λ_b", is not well defined on merged
statistics as stated, and I stopped rather than pick an interpretation.**

**Measured from `AnalysisScripts/GeneratedPairRegistry.h` (300 rows parsed):**

| | |
|---|---|
| distinct **trigger** species | **12** — `±411, ±421, ±4122` (charm), `±521, ±511, ±5122` (beauty) |
| distinct **associate** species | 50 |
| **Σ_b as a trigger** | **absent** |
| **Σ_b as an associate** | `±5222, ±5212, ±5112` — **6 pair files each, 36 of 300** |
| Σ_b excited states `5224/5214/5114` | **absent entirely** |
| Λ_b | present as a **trigger** (`±5122`) |

**Why this blocks a number rather than just qualifying one.** Σ_b is only
measurable as an **associate yield conditional on a heavy-flavour trigger**;
Λ_b is available as a **trigger yield**. A ratio of the two is not a ratio of
like quantities, and quoting one would embed an unstated conditioning.

**The defensible forms, for the owner to choose:**

1. **Both associate-side at fixed trigger** — for each trigger species, associate
   Σ_b / associate Λ_b. Well defined, directly available, but **conditional on
   the trigger** and must be labelled that way.
2. **Both trigger-side** — impossible; Σ_b is not a trigger.
3. **Σ_b associate vs Λ_b trigger** — **do not do this.** It is the reading the
   brief's phrasing invites and it silently mixes conditioned and unconditioned
   yields.

**A second definitional caveat, on the baryon fraction.** It *is* computable
without ambiguity from the trigger side — baryons `±4122, ±5122` against mesons
`±411, ±421, ±521, ±511` — but that is a **baryon fraction within the 12
registered trigger species**, not a true inclusive fraction: Ξc, Ωc, Ξb and
friends are not in the registry. **Report it with that scope in its name**, or
it will be read as inclusive.

**Where the numbers would come from once the definition is fixed.**
`hFlavourClosureSummary` bin 1 is "weighted triggers" per trigger PDG
(`status_analysis_THnSparse_qq.C:1127-1135`), written once per trigger species
and duplicated across pair files sharing that trigger — so one representative
file per trigger PDG suffices. SEM comes from the ten promoted blocks per tune.
**Nothing has been computed. No contrast numbers exist yet.**

**Correction to the brief:** `AuditTuneSettings.C` is at
**`Validation/AuditTuneSettings.C`**, not `AnalysisScripts/`.
