### 2b. Do NOT read "8/8 retries succeeded" as evidence the hang is seed-specific

Tempting and wrong. Under a constant 2.7 % per-job hang rate,
P(0 hangs in 8) = 0.973^8 = **0.80**. This outcome is the *expected* one either
way and discriminates nothing about the mechanism.

What it does support: **convergence is fast.** At 3000 jobs, ~80 hangs -> round
2 leaves ~2 -> round 3 ~0. **Two to three rounds, ~82 seeds.** `MAX_ATTEMPTS =
10` (`campaign.py:69`) is not close to binding.
