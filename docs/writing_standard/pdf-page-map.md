## How to check a word

1. Find the letter in the index above. Read a 2–4 page range from that start
   page.
2. UPPERCASE entry = approved, with its one approved meaning and part of
   speech. Lowercase entry = not approved; the entry gives the approved
   alternative.
3. A word absent from the list can still be correct as a technical noun or
   technical verb (rules 1.5 and 1.12): an exact domain term keeps its place.

## Issue 9 terminology notes

- "Technical noun" fully replaces the older term "technical name".
- Rule 2.3 (old) moved to Section 4 as rule 4.5 (articles and demonstrative
  adjectives).
- GR-7 (inclusive language) and GR-8 (possessive form) are new in Issue 9.
